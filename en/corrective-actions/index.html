Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an incident and a corrective action?

The incident spots the problem; the corrective action solves it. Every incident or audit finding generates an action plan with an owner, a closing date and follow-up until it is resolved.

How is an owner and a date assigned to each action?

The action is created with an owner and a closing date. The owner gets an immediate notification in the app and by email, and the system sends reminders before it falls due.

Does Solved handle audit non-conformities (ISO 9001, IFS, BRC)?

Yes. It logs every audit or inspection non-conformity, assigns it with an owner and a deadline and keeps full traceability from detection to closure. Compatible with ISO 9001, IFS, BRC, ISO 22000 and HACCP.

What is root cause analysis and what is it for?

It is a continuous improvement method aimed at the origin of the deviation rather than its symptom. Solved links every corrective action to its analysis, so the fix is permanent and on record.

Can I schedule planned tasks as well as corrective actions?

Yes. Beyond reactive work, the system lets you schedule recurring or one-off tasks and assign them to departments or people, tracked just like corrective actions.
