What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files used to store small pieces of information. They are saved on your device when the website loads in your browser. These cookies help us make the website work properly, keep it more secure, provide a better user experience, understand how the site performs and analyse what works and where it needs improving.

How do we use cookies?

As with most online services, our website uses first- and third-party cookies for several purposes. First-party cookies are mostly necessary for the website to work properly, and they do not collect any personally identifiable data about you.

What types of cookies do we use?

  • Essential: some cookies are essential for you to experience the full functionality of our site. They let us keep user sessions alive and prevent security threats. They neither collect nor store any personal information.

  • Statistics: these cookies store information such as the number of visitors to the website, the number of unique visitors, which pages have been visited, where the visit came from, and so on. This data helps us understand and analyse how well the website performs and where it needs improving.

How can I control my cookie preferences?

You can manage your cookie preferences by clicking the “Settings” button and enabling or disabling the cookie categories in the pop-up as you prefer.

Besides this, different browsers offer different ways to block and delete the cookies used by websites. You can change your browser settings to block or delete cookies. For more on managing and deleting cookies, visit wikipedia.org, www.allaboutcookies.org.
