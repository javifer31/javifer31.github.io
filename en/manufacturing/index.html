Disconnected processes, with a direct hit on productivity

Many industrial plants run on disconnected processes, unreliable records and informal communication. That does not just slow the work down: it puts quality, safety and compliance at risk.

Processes documented on paper or spreadsheets that get lost, duplicated or reviewed too late.

Faults and incidents spotted late that leave no trace of how they were resolved.

Maintenance badly planned or carried out with no traceability.

Critical documents scattered across folders, uncontrolled and hard to reach.

Training that is not standardised and depends on the previous operator's experience.

One system to coordinate the plant and standardise every process

Solved gives operations a structure, cuts manual error and keeps continuous improvement going on the floor, without relying on paper or improvised builds.

The checks for each production line are completed digitally and are available straight away. Training sits inside the record itself: every check carries its procedure and guides the operator step by step, with no separate manual.

Frequently asked questions

Which industrial sectors is Solved for?

For manufacturing, chemicals, packaging, metal and any industry that needs to standardise and digitise its quality processes.

Does it standardise processes across plants and teams?

Yes. Solved unifies procedures and checklists so every team works to the same standard.

Does it integrate with the systems I already use?

Yes. It connects to the ERP, MES and CMMS already in place to bring information together without duplicating data.

Does it cut errors and improve visibility?

Yes. Digitising records and incidents cuts manual error and gives real-time visibility of the whole operation.
