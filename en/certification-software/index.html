Frequently asked questions

Does it work for more than one standard at once?

Yes, and that is the point of this page. Logging a check, tracing a batch or a corrective action do not change with the certification body: they are done once and answer whichever standard applies.

Does Solved certify my plant?

No. Certifying is a job for an accredited body. What Solved does is carry the system day to day so that, when the audit arrives, the evidence is there and does not have to be manufactured.

What if we are audited against two standards in the same year?

The same record serves both. What changes is the internal audit checklist and which evidence you filter, not the work on the floor.

Does it come with each standard's requirements loaded?

No, and it is worth being precise: Solved does not interpret the standard or replace the consultant. What it adds is that whatever the system documents actually gets done and can be proven.

Does it work for ISO 14001 or ISO 45001?

Yes, with the same mechanics: scheduled check, record with an owner, finding and action through to closure. What changes is what you check, not how you record it.

Do we have to redo the forms we already have approved?

No. They are loaded as they are. What changes is where they get filled in, and that there is now a single current version.
