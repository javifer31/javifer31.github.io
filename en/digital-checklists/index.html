Each check is dragged into place by its handle and takes one of seventeen answer types: number, select, date, calculation, formula, signature or code scan.

A formula decides whether the measured value is within range and resolves it as OK or KO with nobody interpreting it; a visibility rule shows the check only when it applies.

Every template is scheduled with its frequency, its date and its owner, and the module calendar shows at once what is still to do and what has been closed. The alert goes out before the check falls due, not once it already has.

Every closed record keeps its execution date, its status and its evidence, and the history is filtered by production line, shift or product. That is where the records report in Excel comes from, and every signed record, as a PDF.

Frequently asked questions

What is a digital checklist?

It is a verification list filled in from a phone, tablet or PC instead of on paper, automatically recording date, owner and result in real time.

Can I tailor the checklists to each process?

Yes. Solved lets you configure bespoke templates for each check, inspection or procedure, matched to each plant's standards and way of working.

Does Solved help with IFS, BRC or ISO 9001 compliance?

Yes. With records digitised and available at all times, compliance is documented without prep work before the audit.

Can I schedule how often each check runs?

Yes. You set each procedure's frequency and date, and the system notifies and assigns owners so none is left undone.

Does it work on any device?

Yes. Records are filled in and reviewed from a phone, a tablet or a PC.

Does it work for preventive maintenance?

Yes. A preventive job is set up as a scheduled record on the asset, with its frequency and its owner, and a deviation found during the check opens an incident on that same equipment.

Does an out-of-range check open the incident automatically?

Yes. A check with a KO result generates the incident with its owner and its deadline, linked to the record that produced it.

Can the record history be exported?

Yes. The history is filtered by line, shift or product and exported with the attached evidence, and the product's AI also issues tailored reports and each site's weekly report.
