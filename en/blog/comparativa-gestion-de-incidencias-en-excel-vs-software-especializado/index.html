Incident management: Excel vs software

By Claudia · December 3, 2025 · 8 min read

In many plants, the incident management with software has not yet fully replaced the traditional method based on spreadsheets. Excel remains the most common tool due to its low cost and accessibility. However, as industrial operations grow, regulatory requirements increase and teams expand, managing incidents in Excel stops being enough.

Manual record-keeping, data duplication and lack of traceability make plants lose time, productivity and control. Instead, a specialized incident management software makes it possible to automate processes, get real-time information and ensure regulatory compliance with no effort.

In this article we will look at how incidents are managed in Excel, its main limitations, the advantages of professional software, a direct comparison between both approaches and how Solved overcomes all the barriers of the traditional method.

How incidents are managed in Excel

Excel has been the starting point for years for many companies looking to organize their information. The incident management with software may seem like an unnecessary investment when you already have a familiar, free tool like Excel. However, its simplicity is also its greatest weakness.

1. Familiarity and low cost

Excel is widely known by all administrative teams. It requires no advanced training, nor additional licenses in most cases. For a small plant, it is a quick way to start logging incidents and doing basic follow-up.

2. Total flexibility

Each company can adapt its sheets to its processes: custom columns, colors, filters and formulas. This initial freedom gives a sense of control over the data.

3. Ease of sharing files

In small environments, sharing a file by email or internal network may seem enough. However, as the number of incidents grows, this practice becomes a source of errors and wasted time.

In conclusion, Excel works well in early stages, but lacks the structure needed to manage the complexity of a modern industrial plant.

Limitations of Excel in incident management

Although Excel seems practical, it has critical limitations that directly affect traceability, data quality and regulatory compliance. The incident management with specialized software solves precisely these problems.

1. Human errors and duplicates

Incidents must be entered manually, and this increases the likelihood of typing errors, incorrect dates or duplicate records. Also, when several departments use different versions of the same file, the data stops being consistent.

2. Lack of traceability

An Excel sheet does not show who changed a record or when. This is a serious problem during audits, where evidence of tracking for each incident is required. Without traceability, the effectiveness of the quality system cannot be demonstrated.

3. Difficulty in audits

Searching for old incidents, corrective actions or evidence can take hours or even days. The incident management with software eliminates this problem by centralizing all the information and generating instant reports.

4. Inability to work on the plant floor in real time

Excel is designed for office computers, not for the industrial environment. Operators cannot log incidents from their phone or tablet, so data is captured after the problem has already occurred. This creates delays and loss of context.

5. Lack of visibility and analysis

Excel does not allow you to create automatic dashboards or smart alerts. Information stays static and does not help detect patterns or trends. In contrast, the incident management with software shows up-to-date metrics that make it possible to make data-based decisions.

Advantages of specialized software

Implementing a specialized incident management software completely transforms how a plant controls, analyzes and resolves problems. Unlike Excel, these platforms are designed to deliver full traceability, speed and security in every process.

1. Recording from mobile or tablet

Operators can record incidents the exact moment they happen, with photos or videos as evidence. This enables real-time management, avoiding duplication and delays.

2. Digital evidence and automatic traceability
 Every action is logged with date, time and user. This ensures a complete history of the incident, ideal for audits and certifications (ISO, IFS, BRC, etc.).

3. Integration with ERP, MES and CMMS

Specialized software connects incident management with existing business systems, ensuring data consistency. This avoids recording the same information in multiple places, a common problem with Excel.

4. Automatic reports and visual dashboards

The incident management with software makes it possible to generate reports and charts with key indicators (KPIs) in seconds: average resolution time, open incidents, recurring causes or compliance with due dates.

5. Fewer errors and higher productivity

Automating assignments, reminders and workflows eliminates human error and improves response speed. Owners receive automatic alerts for every status change.

6. Simplified regulatory compliance

Thanks to digital traceability and complete documentation, audits are faster and reports are generated automatically with no extra effort.

Hidden costs of using Excel

One of the most common mistakes is thinking Excel is “free”. Although it requires no upfront investment, the lack of automation generates hidden costs that impact profitability and productivity.

1. Time lost consolidating information

Teams spend hours gathering data from different files, checking for duplicates and producing manual reports.

2. Recording errors

A wrong number or a shifted cell can invalidate an entire report. Fixing these errors means rework and loss of confidence in the data.

3. Low visibility

Without dashboards or alerts, owners are unaware of the real status of incidents. This delays decision-making and extends resolution times.

4. Impact on audits

Searching for evidence or demonstrating traceability with Excel files can take days and raise the team's stress. With incident management software, that information is always available with one click.
