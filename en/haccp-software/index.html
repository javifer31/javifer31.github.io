Paper HACCP does not fail at the audit, it fails in the daily routine

The plan is usually written properly. The break appears between the plan and the line: who logged each check, at what time and what was done after a deviation.

CCP reports get filled in retrospectively at the end of the shift, and sometimes the night before the audit.

Paper gets damaged or lost, and a missing record cannot be reconstructed.

The deviation is jotted in the margin of the report, with no record of the correction, the owner or the evidence.

Copying the reports into a spreadsheet to issue the verification eats up days of work every month.

There is no way to check in real time whether a scheduled check was actually done.

From the paper report to a record with time, owner and evidence

Each check in the HACCP plan is set up as a template the operator fills in from a phone or tablet at the workstation. The organisation defines the fields, the frequency and the critical limits.

Templates per check: temperatures, personal hygiene, goods-in, cleaning or metal detector, each with its own fields and limits.

Frequency and owner scheduled: the system notifies before the due date and leaves a record when a check is not done.

Action on a deviation: the record raises an incident with an owner, a deadline and photo evidence through to closure.

Prerequisites in the same system: cleaning and disinfection, pest control, training, water, maintenance and suppliers, each with its history.

Verification without retyping: the history filters by line, shift, product or date and exports on demand.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own check templates?

Yes. The reports in use are replicated unchanged: the same fields, the same critical limits and the same frequency. The HACCP plan does not bend to the software.

What happens when a check goes outside its limits?

The record itself opens a corrective action with an owner, a deadline and evidence, linked to the check that caused it. The later verification does not need reconstructing: it is already done.

Is it useful for preparing an IFS, BRCGS or FSSC 22000 audit?

Yes. Every record carries a date, a time, an owner and evidence, and filters by period, line or product — exactly what the auditor asks for when taking a sample.

How long does it take to get running?

A typical rollout takes two to three weeks and depends on the number of checks, not on the size of the plant: the existing templates are loaded and the rollout starts on one line or one shift.

Does it replace my HACCP consultant?

No. Solved does not write the plan or decide the critical points. What it does is make sure the plan is carried out every day and that it can be proven.
