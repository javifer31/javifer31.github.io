Inefficiencies that eat up shift hours

Plant operations need speed, coordination and reliable data. Without a digital tool that is useful and fits the daily routine, processes turn slow, error-prone and hard to audit.

Paper records are not reviewed in time, which delays decisions.

Incidents are passed on informally, with no traceability and no named owners.

Cleaning and critical control point (CCP) checks have no digital evidence that they were done.

On top of that, preparing for audits is complex and scattered across many documents.

And operational training leans too heavily on what a few key people know.

Record, control and improve without leaving the line.

Solved brings the plant's operational information together: what happens, when it happens and who does it, in a digital form that is accessible and traceable.

The report is opened on the production line itself, with its priority and its photographic evidence, and stays traced from the alert through to closure. However it comes in, it ends up as the same incident.

Every deviation produces an action with an owner and a closing date, and the three teams work on the same circuit: nobody forwards the report or writes it out again for the next shift.

Frequently asked questions

Is Solved suitable for HACCP?

Yes. It digitises HACCP and its prerequisites, with traceable records available at any moment for audits and inspections.

Does it help pass IFS or BRC audits?

Yes. It keeps quality and food safety records current and available, which cuts the work before certification.

Does it enable traceability on the floor?

Yes. The system records checks, batches and verifications, which secures traceability and compliance right through the process.

Does it get paper out of the food plant?

Yes. Everything is logged from a phone or tablet, which removes paper and transcription errors.
