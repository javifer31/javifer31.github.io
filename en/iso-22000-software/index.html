Frequently asked questions

Does Solved certify my plant to ISO 22000?

No. Certification is issued by an accredited body. Solved carries the system's day-to-day running so the evidence exists before the audit.

Does it work for FSSC 22000 too?

Yes. FSSC 22000 is ISO 22000 plus the ISO/TS 22002 series prerequisites and some extra requirements. All of them run as scheduled checks that leave a record.

How does it help with the internal audit?

With your own checklist per clause, findings turned into actions with an owner and a deadline, and a closure traced through to the effectiveness check.

Do we have to redo the system's documents?

No. The forms you have already approved are loaded as they are. What changes is where they get filled in, and that there is now only one current version.

Is it useful if we are not certified yet?

Yes, and it usually works out cheaper: it is easier to build the system recording from the start than to reconstruct a year of evidence the month before the certification audit.
