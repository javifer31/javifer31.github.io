Frequently asked questions

Is it a separate Solved module?

No. It is the document manager, with the record templates and incidents already in use on the floor, applied to suppliers. It is configured on your existing install; no bespoke development needed.

How is it different from a contractor compliance platform?

A contractor coordination platform checks the paperwork of contractors coming on site: safety, insurance, registrations. This page is about approving the supplier of raw material, packaging, spare parts or maintenance services, on quality and food safety criteria.

Who keeps the documents up to date?

Solved notifies the assigned owner ahead of every expiry, and the document is added from a phone or PC the moment it arrives. The alert does not depend on anyone tracking it by hand.

Can suppliers be rated on goods-in incidents?

Yes. Every rejection or incident is tied to its supplier, so the periodic review runs on the actual history rather than on estimates.

Is it useful if we already keep suppliers in the ERP?

Yes. The ERP holds the supplier master data and the purchases; what it does not hold is the quality evidence or the goods-in incidents. Solved covers that part and leaves it ready for the audit.

Can it be used to audit the supplier at their own plant?

Yes. The supplier audit uses the same digital checklist as internal audits: it is filled in at their site from a phone and comes out with findings and actions assigned.
