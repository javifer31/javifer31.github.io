Case study · Meat processing

Two hours a day spent logging incidents by hand.

Ana Domínguez runs quality at Carnavi, a meat processor with more than forty years behind it. In two minutes she explains which channels incidents came through — messaging, email and word of mouth — what happened when management put a number on the cost, and how they are handled now.
