Frequently asked questions

What is Solved?

Solved is industrial operations management software: it brings incidents, control records, documentation and indicators together in a single system, reachable from phone, tablet and PC.

Which companies is Solved for?

For industrial plants — food, chemicals, packaging, general manufacturing — that run their operations today on spreadsheets, paper or messaging apps and need to bring them into a single system.

Does Solved integrate with my ERP, MES or CMMS?

Yes. Solved has connectors for the main ERP, MES and CMMS systems on the market, which avoids duplicated data and keeps management in one place.

Can I use Solved on a phone?

Yes. Solved runs on phone, tablet and PC, so logging and looking things up happen right at the workstation.

How long does implementation take?

A typical rollout takes two to three weeks, with support throughout the go-live.
