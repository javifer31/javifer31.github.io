What syncs, and in which direction

Solved's integration is not about making the ERP, the MES and the CMMS talk to each other: it sits above all three as a cross-cutting layer.

Master data sync: customers, suppliers, products, batches and assets flow from the ERP into Solved and stop being maintained twice.

Incidents flowing back: what is logged and resolved in Solved goes back to the ERP, where the information is consolidated.

Frequently asked questions

Which systems does Solved integrate with?

The full catalogue is on this same page.

Do I have to integrate Solved with my ERP to start using it?

No. Solved runs on its own: it brings the whole plant's incidents, records and actions together without depending on any other system. Integration comes in when it adds value, not as a requirement for starting.

What data syncs between Solved and the ERP?

Downwards, the master data: customers, suppliers, products, batches and assets flow from the ERP into Solved and stop being maintained twice. Upwards, the data from incidents handled in Solved goes back to the ERP to be consolidated.

Who does the initial integration?

Solved's technical team takes on the initial integration with whatever system the organisation already has in place.

What if my system is not on the list?

Solved's open API lets you connect in-house developments or systems that are not in the catalogue. You can ask at info@trysolved.com or through the form on this page.

Can I see Solved's data in Power BI?

Yes. Solved's data can be queried in real time from Power BI, as well as in the platform's own dashboards and reports.

How long does go-live take?

A typical rollout takes two to three weeks, with support throughout. Integrating with the ERP, the MES or the CMMS does not extend it: that is a later phase.
